# TRADEWINDS

A sailing and trading campaign through seven seeded archipelagos. Choose cargo, tack across the wind, deliver deliberately, and spend your earnings at the shipwright. The final Starlight Charter leads through the hunting Storm King.

Run `node serve.ts`, then open http://localhost:8080. No install, build, or internet connection needed.

A/D or arrows steer; W/S or up/down trim full, reefed, or bare sail. Space braces for two seconds, with an eight-second cooldown. Click a port to mark it. P/Escape pauses; M mutes. Enter advances menus; 1–3 choose contracts and 1–6 buy upgrades. Slow below two knots inside a destination ring to deliver. A missed deadline loses payment, not the run; F takes a fallback. Hull zero ends the run.

Screenshots use deterministic `?shot=title|play|victory|defeat&seed=17` scenes. `window.tradewinds.state` exposes the current log and sailing state.
